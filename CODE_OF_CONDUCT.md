# Code of conduct

We want a place where people can work on a technically precise project that touches traditions many people hold dear.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Treat traditions seriously and accurately: name the school, cite the source, do not caricature it, and do not overstate
  what any of them can show.
- Keep claims honest: calculation is not validation. Do not present a rule's output as established fact about a person.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks. Do not post other people's birth data without their consent.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub; maintainers may remove content and restrict participation to
protect the project and its contributors. Adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
