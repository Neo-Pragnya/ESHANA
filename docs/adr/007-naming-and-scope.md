# ADR-007: Naming and scope

**Status.** Expansion accepted. Clearance **open**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Amended by [ADR-011](011-open-source-distribution.md) (2026-11-20).** ESHANA is an open-source tool stack published as libraries, with no commercial launch. The open question here is closed there.

## Decision

Product name **ESHANA**; canonical expansion **Extensible Spatiotemporal Hypersystem for Astroinformatics, Natural Cycles &
Associations**, used identically in every manifest, doc and CLI help. Category: symbolic computation infrastructure, not an
astrology app. Scope: compute, derive, connect, explain, embed; no prediction claims.

## Open

Trademark and naming clearance; confirming `eshana` on PyPI and crates.io and `@neo-pragnya/eshana` on npm. Do not treat any name as
reserved until done (`NAME-01`–`NAME-03`).
