# Vision and mission

**Vision.** Make time, space, numerical structure and symbolic traditions computable through one precise,
transparent, extensible foundation.

**Mission.** Build the open infrastructure that lets developers:

1. **Compute** reproducible astronomical positions and geometric relationships.
2. **Translate** them into tradition-specific chart and symbolic systems.
3. **Connect** entities, timing cycles, rules and correspondences in a knowledge graph.
4. **Explain** what was computed, which rules were applied and where interpretation begins.
5. **Embed** the same engine in Rust, Python, browsers, local applications, AI agents and services.

**North-star promise.** *One input, many explicit lenses; one calculation history, many downstream products.*

## What it is not

- Not an astrology app, a horoscope generator or a prediction service.
- Not a claim that astrology, numerology or Human Design have validated predictive power.
- Not an LLM that fabricates calculations. Numbers come from deterministic code; a language model may summarise
  *attributed* output but must never recompute positions in a prompt.
- Not a compulsory cloud service. It runs locally, offline, with no account.

## The name

ESHANA is a brand transliteration inspired by the Sanskrit *eṣaṇā* (एषणा), seeking or quest. Its canonical expansion
is **Extensible Spatiotemporal Hypersystem for Astroinformatics, Natural Cycles & Associations**; see
[Naming](naming.md). The full design record is the [master plan](../design/master-plan.md).
